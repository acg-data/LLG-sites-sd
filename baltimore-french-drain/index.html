Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What does an interior French drain do?

It captures groundwater beneath the basement or crawlspace slab and directs it to a sump pit before the water can puddle on the floor.

How is an exterior French drain installed?

A trench is excavated, perforated pipe is set in washed gravel and filter fabric, and the system is graded toward daylight or another safe discharge point.

When is a surface drain the right solution?

Channel drains and catch basins are designed for runoff collecting on driveways, patios, walkways, and low areas of the yard.

What can a full waterproofing system include?

Depending on the property, it can combine drain tile, a properly sized sump pump, sealed discharge piping, vapor barriers, and exterior grading corrections.

Can an existing French drain be repaired?

Yes. Camera inspection can locate sediment, roots, damaged pipe, or a failed discharge. The system may be jetted, repaired in sections, or rebuilt where necessary.
