Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to hire someone to build a fence?

Cost depends on the material, total length, height, site conditions, and project requirements. Request an estimate for accurate pricing.

How long does it take to install a fence?

It depends on the size of the job, but many fences can be installed in one to three days.

Do I need concrete for fence posts?

Concrete is the most secure material for setting fence posts, especially in loose or sandy soil.

What type of fence is best?

The best fence depends on your priorities. Vinyl, wood, aluminum, steel, and chain link each offer different benefits.
