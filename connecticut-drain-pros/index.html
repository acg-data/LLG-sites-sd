How do I know which drainage system I need?+

We start with the property, not a product. Our crew checks the grade, soil, foundation and discharge options, then recommends the right fix for the way water is actually moving.

What does a French drain cost?+

Price depends on access, length, depth, soil conditions and the discharge route. We inspect the site and provide a clear written estimate before work begins.

Do you use subcontractors?+

No. Your work is completed by our in-house crew, so quality and accountability stay with Connecticut Drain Pros from assessment through final cleanup.

Do you provide free estimates?+

Yes. We provide free on-site assessments and aim to respond to new enquiries within one business hour.
