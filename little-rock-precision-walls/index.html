Frequently asked questions

What type of retaining wall is the cheapest?+

Concrete block and pressure-treated wood are the most affordable retaining wall materials. Professionally installed 100 sq ft of concrete block typically runs $2,200–$4,000, and timber walls run roughly $2,200–$4,600 depending on height.

Are poured concrete walls better than block walls?+

Both work well when built correctly. Block walls are more affordable and easier to repair section-by-section. Poured concrete is extremely strong, low-maintenance, and a great fit for high-traffic or load-bearing applications.

How deep should a 3-foot retaining wall be?+

For a 3-ft wall in typical Little Rock soil, footings are usually 18–24 inches deep. Loose soils or high water tables can push that to 36 inches.

What are the four basic types of retaining walls?+

Gravity walls, cantilevered walls, sheet pile walls, and anchored walls. We’ll recommend the right type for your site during the free estimate.
