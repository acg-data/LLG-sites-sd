Frequently Asked
Questions

Answers to common questions about retaining-wall planning, materials and installation.

What retaining wall materials do you install?

We install concrete, natural stone, timber, block, Versa-Lok, Belgard and other custom retaining-wall systems.

Do retaining walls need drainage?

Yes. Proper drainage and grading are essential to protecting the wall and preventing pressure buildup behind it.

Can you build a wall on a sloped yard?

Yes. Retaining walls are an effective way to turn a sloped area into stable, usable outdoor space.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Contact us for a free consultation and estimate for your retaining-wall project.
