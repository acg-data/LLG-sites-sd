Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What retaining wall materials do you install?

We design and build concrete, segmental block, natural stone, and timber retaining walls. The best material depends on your site, structural needs, budget, and preferred appearance.

Do you repair existing retaining walls?

Yes. We assess damaged, leaning, or failing walls and provide repair or full-replacement recommendations based on the wall's condition and underlying cause.

Why is drainage important?

Water pressure is a common cause of retaining wall failure. Proper base preparation, drainage, and reinforcement help the wall perform through Charlotte's heavy rain and changing soil conditions.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Charlotte, Concord, Gastonia, Rock Hill, Huntersville, Matthews, Mint Hill, Cornelius, Davidson, Mooresville, Fort Mill, Indian Trail, and nearby Piedmont communities.
