What is the average cost of crawl space encapsulation?+

Cost depends on the size and condition of the crawl space and the materials required. The source site notes that projects commonly average around $7,500, with a broad range from roughly $5,000 to $30,000. A detailed inspection is the best way to establish an accurate price.

Is crawl space encapsulation worth the cost?+

Encapsulation can create a drier, more stable environment, discourage mold growth, improve comfort and energy performance, deter pests and help protect the foundation of your home.

What should I know before encapsulating a crawl space?+

Good materials and correct installation matter. An improperly sealed system can trap moisture, and the vapor barrier must be protected from tears. Access for future repairs and inspections should also be considered from the start.

Is sealing a crawl space always the right solution?+

Not always. A very wet or damaged space may first need drainage, repair or professional moisture assessment. Depending on the home, the right plan may combine a drainage system, sump pump, dehumidifier, repairs and encapsulation.
