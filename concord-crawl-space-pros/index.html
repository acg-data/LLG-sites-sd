Why It Matters

Protect Your Home From the Ground Up

A neglected crawl space affects your entire home—from structure to energy bills to indoor air quality.

01

Mold & Poor Air Quality

Up to half the air in your home can come from the crawl space—damp air means unhealthy air.

02

High Energy Bills

Moisture and poor insulation force your HVAC system to work harder, all year long.

03

Structural Damage

Untreated moisture leads to wood rot and foundation damage that only gets more expensive over time.
