Frequently asked questions

What types of fences do you install?

Youngstown Fence Pros installs wood, vinyl and chain link fences, along with pedestrian and driveway gates. We also repair many existing fence styles.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Call, email or use the estimate section below to tell us about your project and request a free estimate.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Youngstown and surrounding Mahoning Valley communities. Contact us to confirm service for your property.

Can you repair an existing fence or gate?

Yes. We handle common repairs including leaning posts, broken boards, damaged panels, loose hardware and gates that no longer open or close correctly.

How do I get started?

Call (330) 555-0168 or email mike@youngstownfencepros.com. Share the fence type, approximate size and what you want the project to accomplish.
