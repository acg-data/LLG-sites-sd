Crawl Space Questions, Answered

Practical guidance for a healthier, better-protected home

?

What does crawl space encapsulation cost?

A typical Cary project can range from about $2,000 to $5,500, while large or complex spaces may cost more. Materials, size, moisture conditions, and installation needs all affect the final estimate.

?

Is crawl space encapsulation worth it?

Encapsulation helps keep moisture and pests out while protecting structural integrity. It can also support better indoor air quality, making it a valuable long-term investment in your home.

?

How long can encapsulation last?

A quality encapsulation system can last roughly 15 to 20 years—and sometimes longer—with good waterproofing materials, professional installation, and regular inspections.
